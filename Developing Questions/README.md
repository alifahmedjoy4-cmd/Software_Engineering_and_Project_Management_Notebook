## 🧩 Unique Design & Model

**Design principles**
- 🔹 **One topic = one question.** No sub-parts, so each question tests one clear outcome.
- 🔹 **Triple tagging.** Each question carries `[Marks, CO, Bloom]`.
- 🔹 **Traceable.** Each topic lists the PDF pages where the answer is found.
- 🔹 **Balanced.** C1 = 19 marks, C2 = 21 marks; all 5 COs covered.
- 🔹 **Time-fit.** 40 marks for 60 minutes, about 1.5 min per mark.

**Model (flow)**


## 📝 Question Paper

**Full Marks: 40 · Time: 1 Hour · Answer all questions**

**Q1. [4 marks, CO1, C1]** Define software design. Name the two basic steps of the design process and state the outcome of the process, with the reason for recording decision rationale.

**Q2. [6 marks, CO1, C2]** Explain coupling and cohesion. Why is a good design called "low coupling, high cohesion"? Give a short example of a good and a bad split of a component.

**Q3. [5 marks, CO2, C1]** Define the CK metrics CBO, WMC, RFC, DIT and NOC with their formulas.

**Q4. [5 marks, CO2, C1]** Define LCOM (using the sets P and Q), LCOM2 and LCOM3 with their formulas. State which values are best and worst.

**Q5. [5 marks, CO3, C2]** Define software architecture. Explain any three architectural styles (layered, event-based, MVC, blackboard, pipes and filters) with one example each.

**Q6. [5 marks, CO4, C1]** What is a design pattern? Name the three GoF categories and list at least three patterns under each.

**Q7. [5 marks, CO4, C2]** Explain the Strategy pattern: its intent, structure (Strategy, ConcreteStrategy, Context), benefits and liabilities.

**Q8. [5 marks, CO5, C2]** Explain the roles of Model, View and Controller in MVC. Differentiate Page Controller from Front Controller and say when each works best.
